### Data and methodology

- **Fingerprint twins across random splits** (`scripts/02_clean_data.py:21-28`):
  3.5-20.2% of random-split test molecules have a bit-identical 2,048-bit
  Morgan fingerprint in train (hivpr 104/515, vegfr2 68/1,929). Most are

  homologues; most pairs carry different labels (mean |ΔpIC50| 0.30-0.65).
